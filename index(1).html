Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Crispy on the edges, chewy in the middle, and packed with chocolate chips. A simple classic made for sharing.

Prep15 min
Cook12 min
Total27 min
Serves24 cookies
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

02 / METHOD

Instructions

  1. 01

    Preheat oven. Set oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.

  2. 02

    Mix dry ingredients. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Cream butter and sugars. In a large bowl, beat softened butter with both sugars until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.

  4. 04

    Add eggs and vanilla. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract.

  5. 05

    Combine wet and dry. Gradually blend in the flour mixture until just combined. Do not overmix.

  6. 06

    Fold in chocolate chips. Stir in chocolate chips and nuts, if using.

  7. 07

    Shape and bake. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto prepared baking sheets, spacing them 2 inches apart. Bake 9–11 minutes until golden brown.

  8. 08

    Cool. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack.
